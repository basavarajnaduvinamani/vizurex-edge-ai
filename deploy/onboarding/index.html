SHOP-FLOOR WORKFLOW PROTOTYPE

Capture a finding.
Close the loop.

Prototype · work in progress (Oct–Dec 2026)

Built at WCTE (Walmart Centre for Tech Excellence) · Wadhwani School of Data Science & AI · IIT Madras

VisureX is a smart-glasses + phone platform prototype for MSME shop floors. It brings visual inspection, audit walks and quality follow-up into one local workflow.

Intended to help make everyday quality and operations work more reliable across WCTE’s network of 50+ MSMEs.* No factory pilot or user-adoption result is claimed.

CAPTURE → STRUCTURE → ROUTE → CLOSE → LEARN

Photo or voice note → finding or NCR draft → owner and due date → after-photo and CAPA closure → local plant and demo-network patterns.

Demo data: Plant A and Plant B are fictional. Your records and redacted photos stay in IndexedDB on this browser/device. There is no cloud sync.

  1. InspectLive prototype

    Teach a part with eight references plus normal calibration; check a new photo or request a RETAKE.

  2. WalkLive prototype

    Gemba, 5S, safety and layered process audit checklists; voice or typed notes become reviewable findings.

  3. NCRLive prototype

    Draft, submit, disposition, CAPA and closure, with local role checks and an append-only audit log.

  4. ActionsLive prototype

    Owner roles, due dates, overdue escalation flags and after-photo closure.

  5. Plant and network viewLive prototype · demo data

    Counts of local findings, NCRs and closures; network view shows aggregates only.

  6. Glasses captureIn progress

    Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 bridge; real pairing, frame capture and spoken feedback remain unverified.

  7. Further workflowsPlanned

    Incoming lots, shift handover, gauge/display reading, SOP step guidance and PO/MANGO integrations.

Prototype · work in progress (Oct–Dec 2026)

Built at WCTE (Walmart Centre for Tech Excellence) · Wadhwani School of Data Science & AI · IIT Madras

Build a normal-image memory bank, then check a new image. Inference runs on this device. Your photos stay in this browser.

Eight references plus separate normal calibration photos. This is a prototype, not a validated factory inspection system.

01 Teach

Ready. The pretrained model loads only when you build the bank.

DINOv2 ViT-S/14, WASM CPU. Initial download is about 88 MB; loading and onboarding take longer than a warm check. Upload thresholds are estimated from your normal photos and are not validated.

02 Check

Build a memory bank before inspecting.

Heatmap: cosine patch distance on a fixed 0–1 scale. Warm request latency includes image preprocessing and scoring; model loading is excluded. These are generated examples; misses and false alarms remain visible.

03 Retake when needed

Darkness, overexposure or too little detail can request a RETAKE before scoring. Passing this check does not establish image quality. The earlier blur stress study passed blurred captures and produced false alarms.

Read the capture-check limitations

What the benchmark measured

15 MVTec AD categories, three reference-selection seeds. Extra normal calibration images are required beyond k. AUROC* is ranking quality, not the probability that a particular verdict is correct.

These are benchmarked and studied baselines, not a new anomaly-detection method. AnomalyDINO — Damm et al., WACV 2025 · Official code · PatchCore — Roth et al., CVPR 2022.

*AUROC: area under the receiver operating characteristic curve. *k-shot: k normal reference images. *ONNX: Open Neural Network Exchange. Intervals describe category variation on this benchmark, not factory generalisation.
