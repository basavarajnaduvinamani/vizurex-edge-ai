01 Teach

Ready. The pretrained model loads only when you build the bank.

DINOv2 ViT-S/14, WASM CPU. Initial download is about 88 MB; loading and onboarding take longer than a warm check. Upload thresholds are estimated from your normal photos and are not validated.

02 Check

Build a memory bank before inspecting.

Heatmap: cosine patch distance on a fixed 0–1 scale. Warm request latency includes image preprocessing and scoring; model loading is excluded. These are generated examples; misses and false alarms remain visible.

03 Retake when needed

Darkness, overexposure or too little detail can request a RETAKE before scoring. Passing this check does not establish image quality. The earlier blur stress study passed blurred captures and produced false alarms.

What the benchmark measured

15 MVTec AD categories, three reference-selection seeds. Extra normal calibration images are required beyond k. AUROC* is ranking quality, not the probability that a particular verdict is correct.

These are benchmarked and studied baselines, not a new anomaly-detection method. AnomalyDINO — Damm et al., WACV 2025 · Official code · PatchCore — Roth et al., CVPR 2022.

*AUROC: area under the receiver operating characteristic curve. *k-shot: k normal reference images. *ONNX: Open Neural Network Exchange. Intervals describe category variation on this benchmark, not factory generalisation.
