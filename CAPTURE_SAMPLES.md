# Sample capture plan — pending owner photos

Date: 2026-11-04. Do not photograph or distribute WCTE/client parts.
Until these arrive the demo uses S5 generated washer drawings, labelled
“generated sample”. No MVTec images may enter this demo.

Choose two or three objects you own:

| Part | Normal condition | Safe demonstration changes |
|---|---|---|
| Plastic bottle cap | Same type, intact rim and top | Removable tape or drawn mark on a spare cap; real damage only on a disposable spare |
| Household screw | Same specification, intact head | Removable mark, missing companion washer, or a genuinely damaged spare |
| Printed label on a box | Correct, legible label in the same position | Removable smudge, partly covered character or displaced label |

Describe staged changes as staged; a tape mark is not an industrial crack.
Use several physical examples where possible; count unique objects and capture
sessions. With only one object, clearly label the sample as one-object and
underpowered. Do not present repeated views as independent parts.

For EACH part collect, in distinct sessions assigned before scoring:
- 8 normal reference photos: `part/ref/ref_01.jpg` … `ref_08.jpg`.
- 8 additional normal calibration photos: `part/cal/cal_01.jpg` … `cal_08.jpg`.
- About 10 normal check photos: `part/dev/normal_01.jpg` … `normal_02.jpg`;
  `part/test/normal_03.jpg` … `normal_10.jpg`.
- About 10 changed check photos: `part/dev/change_01.jpg` … `change_02.jpg`;
  `part/test/change_03.jpg` … `change_10.jpg`.

The separate calibration photos are additional to the eight reference photos.
This totals 36 photos per part by design, not a completed collection.
The 2+2 development examples check framing and code only. Freeze their outcome,
file list, model version and threshold before opening the 8+8 final checks.
No editing cutoffs after seeing final checks. Any later revision needs a new
untouched collection. Even eight calibration examples cannot establish a
reliable extreme percentile or factory reliability; the demo remains unvalidated.

Use a plain matte mid-tone background, diffuse daylight or steady room light;
avoid glossy desks, harsh reflections and changing background objects. Hold
the phone steady with the part occupying most of the central square; keep
distance/orientation consistent with reference views. Capture different
ordinary views, not bursts of the same frame. Use the rear camera, default
photo mode, no filters/portrait mode/beautification, digital zoom or flash.
Keep original JPEG/PNG files and resolution (at least 224px each side).
Do not retouch defects. Record phone model/settings, session, physical-object
ID, intended change and label in `manifest.csv` before inference.

Keep personal information, faces, location details and serial numbers out of
images. Strip EXIF/location metadata from copies intended for the demo while
keeping originals private. Confirm ownership and permission before publishing.
We will record every outcome, including missed changes, false alarms and
RETAKEs. No sample size here supports a 100% reliability claim.
